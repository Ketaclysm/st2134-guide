Syllabus limits

Use only ST2134-level material: sufficiency, minimal sufficiency, likelihood, log-likelihood, score function, Fisher information, bias, variance, MSE, consistency, Rao-Blackwell theorem, method of moments, order statistics, CRLB, MVUE, MLE, asymptotic MLEs, pivotal functions, confidence intervals, Neyman-Pearson lemma, most powerful tests, likelihood ratio tests, and nuisance parameters.
